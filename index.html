Who I Am

Hi! I'm Kanachai Niyomsilpchai, an enterprise developer with over 10 years in ASP.NET and 7 years delivering ERP solutions on NetSuite. Beyond writing code, ERP work has trained a strong analytical thinking approach — mapping end-to-end business processes, identifying gaps between what the business needs and what the system does, and translating complex requirements into working solutions across finance, inventory, and operations.

I'm currently bridging from classic ASP.NET to .NET Core — modernizing patterns I already know well into the current ecosystem. Alongside that, I've been building personal projects in Go and Next.js out of genuine curiosity. These are still growing skills with no production experience yet, but investing in them deliberately.

I care about understanding the problem before touching the code. Always open to interesting problems and new challenges.
